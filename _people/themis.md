bio: Themis Halka is a PhD student at the Computational Genomics Research Lab within the Data Science and AI division, department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chalmers University of Technology. She previously completed her master’s degree in Molecular and Cellular Biology, specialising in Data Analysis and AI, at Sorbonne University and Ecole Normale Superieure in France. Previously, she graduated from her bachelors in Medical Biosciences at Imperial College London. Her research interests include human genetic variation and the functional interpretation of genomic data using machine learning tools. At the moment, she focuses on uncovering genetic variant effects with genomic language models.
